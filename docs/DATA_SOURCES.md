# Data sources and licences

Every model and dataset considered for training, with its licence and the decision. Checked on 2026-10-01 from each source's own card or terms. A source is used only if its licence allows fine-tuning and publishing the result, and if its text was not generated by a model whose terms forbid training other models.

## Training data generator (teacher)

| Model | Licence | Outputs may train another model? | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (via OpenRouter, DeepInfra) | MIT (open weights) | Yes: DeepSeek's terms list "training other models (such as model distillation)" as a permitted use | **Used** as the teacher |
| GPT-5.4 mini, Gemini 3.8 Flash | provider terms | No: their terms forbid using outputs to develop competing models | Benchmark competitors only, never training data |
| Claude models | Anthropic commercial terms | No: "train competing AI models" is not allowed | Not used for training data; the test drafts came from this family, so the training data deliberately comes from a different one |

## Public Saudi-dialect datasets

| Dataset | Licence | What it is | Provenance | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [VohoAI/voho-saudi-dialogues](https://huggingface.co/datasets/VohoAI/voho-saudi-dialogues) | Apache-2.0 ("use it commercially, fine-tune on it, redistribute it") | 13,156 Najdi dialogues (7,603 service calls incl. a receptionist role, 5,553 everyday), about 7 words per reply, 12 MB | "Voho's own", written in a spoken register; the card does not say whether a model helped write it | **Candidate** for the dialect mix, pending the pilot |
| [Omartificial-Intelligence-Space/FineWeb2-Najdi-Arabic](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Omartificial-Intelligence-Space/FineWeb2-Najdi-Arabic) | ODC-By (from FineWeb2, Common Crawl) | Web pages classified as Najdi, 2.8 GB | Crawled web text | **Deferred**: raw web text, not conversations; only for a possible "dialect-first" experiment |
| [HeshamHaroon/saudi-dialect-conversations](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HeshamHaroon/saudi-dialect-conversations) | Apache-2.0 label | 3,545 Najdi conversations | "Generation: GPT-4o" | **Excluded**: generated by a model whose terms forbid training other models |
| [AdaMLLab/smolkalam-arabic-conversational-sft](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AdaMLLab/smolkalam-arabic-conversational-sft) | Apache-2.0 | 1.8M general SFT examples | Machine translation of SmolTalk2 into Modern Standard Arabic | **Excluded**: MSA, the opposite of our goal |
| [AhmedSSabir/Gulf-Arabic-Tweets-2018-2020](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AhmedSSabir/Gulf-Arabic-Tweets-2018-2020), [amgadhasan/arabic_tweets_dialects](https://huggingface.co/datasets/amgadhasan/arabic_tweets_dialects) | CC-BY-4.0 / MIT labels | Tweets | Scraped from Twitter/X | **Excluded**: the platform's terms restrict reuse of tweet text |
| [fr3on/arabic-dialect-corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/fr3on/arabic-dialect-corpus) | MIT label | 150K+ Egyptian and Saudi comments | Scraped from YouTube comments | **Excluded**: scraped user comments; the uploader cannot license them |
| [dev-hussein/saudi-arabic-cs-conversations](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dev-hussein/saudi-arabic-cs-conversations) | Apache-2.0 | 100 synthetic customer-service conversations | Free sample of a paid dataset, generator not stated | **Excluded**: too small to matter |
| Datasets with no licence stated (e.g. `karimBD/saudi1`, `oddadmix/dialectal-arabic-lahgtna-v2`, `Mans1611/Lahgtna-saudi`, `SaudiTalk/SaudiTalk`, SADA re-uploads) | none | various | various | **Excluded**: no licence means no permission |
| Non-commercial licences (e.g. `SADA-Najdi-from-kaggle` CC-BY-NC-SA) | NC | various | various | **Excluded**: non-commercial only |

## Never used

Nothing from Mujawib (its code, prompts, rules, evaluation cases or outputs) is used in the test set or the training data. The one exception is the Stage 2 tokenizer probe, which only measured tokenizers, is kept out of git, and never touches training or testing.
